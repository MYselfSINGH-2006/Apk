How to use
  • Drag the round menu button anywhere. Tap it to open or close this panel. Drag this panel by its top bar.
  • Drag the center box to move the circle.
  • Each line goes straight to its hole epicenter (purple).
  • Double-tap a hole to choose its epic angle. Every real angle to that hole appears as a faint line. Touch the one you want: it bounces off the walls and still ends on the epicenter (orange). Double-tap the hole again to finish.
  • Obstacles: drag them anywhere. No line touches one; a blocked line goes round by itself. Double-tap an obstacle to remove it.
